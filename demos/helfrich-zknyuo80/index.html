Your project path

Three steps, and what to ask at each

The path is simple. Open a step to see questions worth asking, so you know what to expect before you commit.

  1. Call or email

    Call (555) 555-0100 [placeholder] or send an email and tell us what you need.

    Worth having ready
    • Which rooms or areas, and what you want done
    • A few photos of the space
    • Your rough measurements (the calculator below helps)

  2. Free estimate

    We look at the job and give you a free estimate.

    Worth asking
    • What does the estimate include, and what is left out?
    • How are measurements confirmed?
    • When can the work be scheduled?

  3. The job done

    We do the work.

    Worth asking
    • How will you hear about schedule changes?
    • What should be moved or cleared before work starts?
    • How is the finished job checked with you?

These are questions for you to ask, not promises. Gregory answers them on the call.

Good to know

Common questions

General questions about getting an estimate. For anything specific to your project, call or email.

How does getting an estimate work?
Call or email and tell us about your project. Then we set up a free estimate. The steps above show the process: call, free estimate, the job done.
What should I have ready?
It helps to have photos of the space, rough measurements, a floor type or two you want to ask about, and any questions. The Prep tab in the planning tools has a checklist and builds a summary you can copy.
Do I need exact measurements?
Rough measurements are enough to start the conversation. The square footage calculator gives you a number to share. Ask how final measurements are handled.
I do not know which floor to choose. Is that okay?
Yes. The picker shows general floor types that are worth asking about for your room. It is general guidance, so talk it over when you call.
What kinds of projects do you take on?
General construction, roofing, siding and windows, with flooring as the main focus right now. If your job does not fit one trade, call or email and describe it.
